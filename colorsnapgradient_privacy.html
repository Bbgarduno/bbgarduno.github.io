ColorSnap Gradient – Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

ColorSnap Gradient does not collect, store, or share any personal information. The app is designed to be used without creating an account, and no personal data is ever required to play.

Information We Collect

The app itself does not collect any personal data. However, we use certain third‑party services that may collect limited, non‑personal information automatically.

Third‑Party Services

Google AdMob

ColorSnap Gradient uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers, approximate location, and usage data for the purpose of delivering personalized or non‑personalized ads.

You can learn more about Google’s data practices here:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

RevenueCat

ColorSnap Gradient uses RevenueCat to manage in‑app purchases. RevenueCat does not collect personal information such as your name, email, or address. It only receives anonymous purchase information from Apple (such as whether a purchase was successful) to unlock features within the app.

You can learn more about RevenueCat’s privacy practices here:
https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

In‑App Purchases

The app includes a single non‑consumable in‑app purchase to unlock Fill Mode. All purchases are processed securely by Apple. We do not have access to your payment information.

Children’s Privacy

ColorSnap Gradient is not directed toward children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
